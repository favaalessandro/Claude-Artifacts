💰 FCF (Free Cash Flow)

Metrica #2 - Framework Buffett | "La Verità sul Profitto Reale"

🎯 Definizione e Concetto

FCF = Operating Cash Flow (OCF) - Capital Expenditures (CapEx)

Cosa misura?

FCF misura il denaro reale disponibile per distribuire agli azionisti senza danneggiare il business.

Non è il profitto contabile — è denaro vero, verificabile in banca.

Buffett dice: "FCF è la metrica che conta davvero. Non puoi ingannare il denaro."

💡 L'Analogia del Contadino (Buffett)

"Immagina un contadino che raccoglie il grano (ricavi) e paga manodopera, fertilizzante, carburante (costi operativi). Quanto denaro rimane per distribuire o reinvestire? QUELLO è il Free Cash Flow."

Un'azienda potrebbe mostrare $100M di utile ma solo $40M di FCF. Qual è il vero "profitto"? Il FCF di $40M.

⚠️ FCF vs. Net Income

Net Income (Contabile):

  • Può essere manipolato
  • Contiene accruals (non-cash)
  • Usa assunzioni contabili
  • Esempio: $100M NI potrebbe essere solo $60M in cassa reale

✅ FCF (Reale)

Free Cash Flow (Cassa):

  • Non può essere finto
  • È denaro vero in banca
  • Verificabile oggettivamente
  • Esempio: $60M FCF = denaro reale disponibile
🔑 Key Insight:
"Una società potrebbe avere utili incredibili sulla carta ma FCF negativo se la cassa è bloccata in inventario/receivables o se rinveste troppo. FCF rivela il vero status di salute."

📈 Benchmark Buffett per FCF

Standard assoluti di qualità:

Metrica FCF Benchmark Eccellente Adeguato Red Flag
FCF Margin (FCF/Revenue) 🟢 >15% 🟡 10-15% 🔴 <5%
FCF Trend 🟢 Growing 10+ years 🟡 Stable/flat 🔴 Declining or negative
FCF Yield (FCF/Market Cap) 🟢 >5% 🟡 3-5% 🔴 <3%
NI/OCF Ratio 🟢 0.8-1.0 🟡 0.6-1.2 🔴 >1.2 persistent
CapEx/Revenue 🟢 <10% 🟡 10-20% 🔴 >20% or deferred
FCF Positive Years 🟢 Every year 🟡 Most years 🔴 Negative 2+ consecutive

💎 Buffett's FCF Principle

"I want to buy businesses that generate high FCF with minimal reinvestment. If a company makes $100M FCF and only needs to reinvest $30M to maintain growth, that's $70M in true economic profit."

Questo è il fondamento per identificare aziende "cash cow" che crescono gratuitamente.

🚨 Red Flags & Manipolazione

🚨 Red Flag #1: Negative o Declining FCF Despite Growing Earnings

Azienda riporta profitti crescenti MA FCF negativo o in calo. Segno di problemi working capital o CapEx eccessivo.

Segnale: NI +20% ma FCF -10% = PROBLEMA SERIO

Cause Comuni: Receivables non incassati, inventory accumulating, CapEx deferred maintenance nascosto

🚨 Red Flag #2: NI >> OCF Divergenza (Earnings Quality Issue)

Se Net Income SUPERA significativamente OCF (>120% divergence) = earnings NON backed by cash.

Segnale: NI $100M ma OCF $60M (ratio 1.67) = ACCRUALS PROBLEM

Rischio: Possibile manipolazione contabile, working capital trap, unsustainable earnings

🚨 Red Flag #3: High CapEx Masking Low Underlying Profitability

Azienda riporta FCF decente ma su CapEx artificialmente basso (deferred maintenance).

Segnale: CapEx declining while assets aging OR CapEx spike next year crater FCF

Esempio: Retailer defers store maintenance, FCF artificially high this year, maintenance backlog explodes next year

🚨 Red Flag #4: One-Time Cash Events Inflating FCF

Asset sales, tax deferrals, working capital squeezes creano temporary FCF spikes non sustainable.

Segnale: FCF $100M this year but from asset sale, recurring operations only $40M

Test: 3-year average FCF vs. latest year. Significativa divergence = RED FLAG

⚠️ FCF Red Flag Checklist

FCF positivo ogni anno? (no = PROBLEMA)
FCF cresce con revenue? (no = quality issue)
NI/OCF tra 0.8-1.0? (>1.2 = red flag)
CapEx sostenibile? (deferred maintenance = TRAP)
FCF margin >5%? (no = inefficient)

🔍 Framework di Analisi: 6 Step

Step 1️⃣: Calcola FCF Margin (FCF/Revenue %)

Azione: Scopri quale % di ricavi si converte in denaro reale.

Benchmark: 10-15%+ per business di qualità

Esempio: Revenue $1B, FCF $150M = 15% margin ✅

Domanda Critica: "Quanta parte dei ricavi diventa denaro disponibile per azionisti?"

Step 2️⃣: Verify NI vs. OCF (Earnings Quality Check)

Azione: Confronta Net Income a Operating Cash Flow.

Test di Qualità: NI dovrebbe essere 80-100% di OCF

  • ✅ 80-100% = Earnings conservativi, backed by cash
  • 🟡 100-120% = Some accruals, normale ma watch
  • 🔴 >120% = Substantial accruals, PROBLEMATICO

Step 3️⃣: Compare FCF to Net Income (Quality Indicator)

Azione: Calcola FCF/NI ratio.

Interpretazione:

  • ✅ FCF >= NI = Conservative accounting (highest quality)
  • ✅ FCF = 80-100% NI = Normal CapEx, healthy
  • 🟡 FCF = 60-80% NI = High CapEx, growth phase
  • 🔴 FCF < 60% NI = Capital intensive, maybe deferred maintenance

Step 4️⃣: Assess CapEx (Maintenance vs. Growth Split)

Azione: Distinguish tra maintenance e growth capital spending.

Proxy: Maintenance CapEx ≈ Depreciation & Amortization

  • Total CapEx = Maintenance + Growth CapEx
  • Growth CapEx reveals reinvestment requirements
  • High growth CapEx = Future FCF depends on ROI

Step 5️⃣: Analyze FCF Growth vs. Earnings Growth

Azione: Confronta growth rate FCF a growth rate earnings.

Preferred: FCF growth ≈ Earnings growth (authentic quality)

Red Flag: Earnings +20% but FCF -5% = manipolazione or CapEx intensity issue

Step 6️⃣: Assess FCF Sustainability (Long-Term Viability)

Azione: Valuta se FCF è sostenibile o temporary.

Sustainable Sources: Moat (pricing power), low CapEx needs, recurring revenue

Unsustainable Sources: R&D cuts, deferred maintenance, one-time items, stretched receivables

Buffett's Test: "Se dovessi scalare questo business 50%, quanto CapEx servirebbe?"
Answer = Asset-light (basso CapEx) = BUONO; Asset-heavy (alto CapEx) = RISCHIOSO

⚙️ Componenti: OCF e CapEx

Operating Cash Flow (OCF)

Cosa è: Denaro generato dalle operazioni quotidiane PRIMA di reinvestimento.

Come Calcolato:

  • Net Income
  • + Depreciation & Amortization (non-cash)
  • ± Changes in Working Capital
  • = OCF

Significa: Profitto "vero" after accounting adjustments ma before capex

Capital Expenditures (CapEx)

Cosa è: Denaro speso per mantenere e espandere asset.

Categorie:

  • Maintenance CapEx: Tenere business at current level
  • Growth CapEx: Espandere operazioni, acquisire asset

Trade-off: CapEx basso = FCF alto NOW ma rischio futuro di obsolescence

📊 La Relazione Fondamentale

FCF POSITIVO (Healthy)

  • OCF > CapEx = Business generat cash after reinvestment
  • Can distribute to shareholders OR reinvest in growth
  • Example: OCF $100M, CapEx $30M, FCF $70M available

FCF NEGATIVE (Problem)

  • CapEx > OCF = Business burning cash despite profits
  • Must fund from debt/equity = unsustainable
  • Example: OCF $50M, CapEx $80M, FCF -$30M (problem!)
⚠️ Critical Insight:
"High-quality businesses have OCF > CapEx by large margin. Poor businesses struggle to generate OCF OR have bloated CapEx needs."

✨ FCF Quality Assessment Test

🟢 Signs of High-Quality FCF

  • ✅ FCF positive every year (not just some years)
  • ✅ FCF growing consistently (not volatile)
  • ✅ FCF margin stable or improving (>10%)
  • ✅ NI backed by cash (NI/OCF 0.8-1.0)
  • ✅ CapEx low relative to OCF (<10% revenue)
  • ✅ Can predict future FCF with confidence
  • ✅ Moat evident (pricing power, low reinvestment needs)

🔴 Signs of Low-Quality FCF

  • ❌ FCF negative or declining multiple years
  • ❌ FCF volatile (unpredictable)
  • ❌ FCF margin low or declining (<5%)
  • ❌ NI >> OCF (earnings not backed by cash)
  • ❌ CapEx bloated or deferred (hidden problems)
  • ❌ Can't predict future FCF reliably
  • ❌ One-time items inflating FCF

🎯 Casi Reali: FCF Quality

✅ Case 1: Apple - Exceptional FCF Quality

🟢 GREEN - FORTRESS CASH
OCF: $122B (huge cash generation)
CapEx: $12B (only 10% of OCF)
FCF: $110B (exceptional)
FCF Margin: 29% (outstanding)
FCF/NI: 110% (cash exceeds earnings)
Trend: Growing consistently

What It Means:

🟢 Apple generates massive real cash with minimal reinvestment. Moat so strong it needs little CapEx to maintain growth. This is the gold standard of FCF quality.

🟡 Case 2: Amazon - Negative FCF (Growth Phase, Acceptable)

🟡 YELLOW - GROWTH INVESTMENT
OCF: $50B
CapEx: $60B (data centers, logistics)
FCF: -$10B (NEGATIVE)
Context: Heavy infrastructure investment phase
Question: Will $60B CapEx convert to future FCF?

Assessment:

🟡 Negative FCF is NOT automatically bad. Amazon invests heavily for future scale. KEY TEST: Does it convert to positive FCF in 3-5 years? If yes, justified. If no, problem.

Action: Monitor quarterly. If CapEx peaks and FCF turns positive = strategy working. If CapEx remains high and FCF stays negative = RED FLAG.

❌ Case 3: Struggling Retailer - FCF Collapse

🔴 RED - CASH BURN
OCF: $30M (declining)
CapEx: $50M (deferred → suddenly spike)
FCF: -$20M (NEGATIVE)
Trend: FCF worsening 3+ years
NI/OCF: 1.3 (working capital issue)

Red Flag Assessment:

🔴 Unlike Amazon (growth investment with evident ROI potential), this retailer's negative FCF is from operational deterioration. OCF declining, CapEx deferred (aging stores), no clear path to positive FCF.

Risk: Will need debt or equity raise to fund operations. Equity destruction likely.

✅ Case 4: Software Company - High-Quality Recurring FCF

🟢 GREEN - SUSTAINABLE
OCF: $150M (strong)
CapEx: $30M (20% of OCF)
FCF: $120M (excellent)
FCF Margin: 24% (very high)
Business Model: SaaS recurring revenue
Trend: FCF growing 10+ years

Why HIGH QUALITY:

🟢 SaaS business model = recurring revenue = predictable FCF. Low CapEx needs (cloud infrastructure leverage). FCF growing consistently. This is the kind of FCF Buffett loves: predictable, growing, backed by moat (customer switching costs).

❓ 7 Domande Critiche per FCF

1. FCF è positivo ogni anno o volatile?

Perché: Positivo ogni anno = business model sano; volatile = ciclico o problematico

2. FCF cresce con revenue o in lag?

Perché: Parallel growth = quality; lagging = working capital or CapEx intensity

3. NI è backed da cash (NI/OCF 0.8-1.0)?

Perché: NI puede be manipulated; OCF è reale. Divergenza = red flag

4. FCF Margin è sano (>5-10%)?

Perché: Rivela cash conversion efficiency. Basso margin = capital intensive o inefficiente

5. CapEx è maintenance o growth deferred?

Perché: Deferred maintenance = future FCF risk. Growth CapEx = test ROI next 3-5 years

6. FCF Yield è attraente (>3-5%)?

Perché: Alta yield = valutazione cheap. Bassa yield = overpriced unless growth high

7. Se dovessi scalare business 50%, quanto CapEx servirebbe?

Perché: Asset-light (basso CapEx) = moat forte. Asset-heavy (alto CapEx) = rischio

💡 Meta-Question:
"Preferisci un'azienda con NI $100M e FCF $50M, o NI $80M e FCF $90M?"
Risposta: La seconda. FCF è il vero profitto. La prima ha problemi working capital nascosti.
