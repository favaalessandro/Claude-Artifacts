What is this board?

"Value Investing: The Thread" was a WWWBoard-powered discussion board hosted under The Market Wizard at sealpoint.com. The surviving capture covers 1-3 May 1997. Michael Burry started the board and posted most of its opening topics; Alan Siegal and Clarke replied.

Why can I not read any of the messages?

Only the index page survives in the source this site was rebuilt from. It records each message's subject, author and timestamp, and the shape of the reply tree, but none of the message bodies. Each message page therefore shows the real header and an explicit note in place of the text. Nothing has been written in to stand for the missing posts.

Some message numbers are missing. Why?

The board numbered messages sequentially. The index lists 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 16, 17 and 18; numbers 3 and 9 through 15 do not appear. On a WWWBoard, that normally means those posts were removed by the board's owner, or were posted to a part of the board the capture did not include. Message 2 is a further case: its counter reports one follow up, but that follow up is not in the index either.

Can I post a message?

No. The original form submitted to a CGI script at www.sealpoint.com/cgi-bin/web541f1/wwwboard.cgi, which has not existed for a very long time. The form is kept because it was part of the page; submitting it takes you to a local notice instead of anywhere on the network.

Who wrote the board software?

WWWBoard was written by Matt Wright and distributed through Matt's Script Archive. It was one of the most widely installed CGI scripts on the web in the mid-1990s.
