What is authentic here

  • The index page markup, reproduced from the archived HTML source: the title, the EckmannD headings, the colour attributes, the WWWBoard threading comments (<!--top: n-->, <!--insert: n-->) and the posting form fields.
  • Every subject line, author name, timestamp and reply-count in the thread listing.
  • The reply structure: which message answers which.

What was reconstructed

  • The message pages. The source links to messages/1.html and its siblings, but those pages are not in it. Each has been rebuilt in the WWWBoard page layout, carrying the real header from the index and an explicit note where the body would be. No message text has been invented.
  • bg-stars.gif. The original background image was not in the source. The file here is a generated starfield standing in for it.
  • faq.html and this page. The source links to a FAQ that was not captured; the FAQ here describes this reconstruction rather than claiming to be the 1997 one.
  • The form target. Changed from the dead sealpoint.com CGI to a local notice page, so that submitting sends nothing over the network.
  • The "The Market Wizard Home" link, which pointed at a site that no longer exists.

Source

Rebuilt from a PDF capture of the page source of Value Investing: The Thread, The Market Wizard, sealpoint.com, showing the board as of 3 May 1997. Board software: WWWBoard by Matt Wright.
